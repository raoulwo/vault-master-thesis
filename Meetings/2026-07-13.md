### Hypotheses


 *H1*. Under real-time VR/AR streaming QoE requirements of latency $\le 15\text{ms}$, container-based live migration schemes such as [[@govindaraj2018|redundancy migration]] with an average service downtime of $\approx 1.68\text{s}$ lead to a linearly growing packet buffer on the destination node during the migration window, approaching hardware RAM limits of satellite edge compute nodes.
